Pancake Recipe

Pancakes are a great way to start your mornings with sweet syrup and butter. A simple staple to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ Flour
  • 2 Tbsp Sugar
  • 1 Tbsp Baking Powder
  • ½ Tsp Salt
  • 1 ¼ Cups Milk
  • 1 Large Egg
  • 3 Tbsps unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 Tsp Vanilla Extract

Procedure

  1. Mix the dry ingredients (Flour/Sugar/Baking Powder/Salt) in a bowl until combined. Make sure there are no lumps.
  2. Mix the wet ingredients (Unsalted Melted Butter/Vanilla Extract/Large Egg/Milk) in a separate bowl.
  3. Mix the wet and dry ingredients to form a batter. Don't overmix.
  4. Heat up your pan and add butter. Make sure to not get the butter burnt.
  5. Put the pancake batter and let it cook until the edges are crispy and there's holes forming. When there is, it is time to flip.
  6. Serve on a plate and drizzle honey or condensed milk (optional) while it's hot.
  7. Enjoy!
